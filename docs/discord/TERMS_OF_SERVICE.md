## 2. What the App does

The App records moderation-relevant events in a Discord server (member joins and leaves, kicks, bans,
role and nickname changes, message deletions and edits, channel and server setting changes, invite
usage, and voice activity) and posts them to log channels chosen by the server's administrators.
